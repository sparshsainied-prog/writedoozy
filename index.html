Half of Hartwell Hotel

When Olivia Peterson randomly inherits half a hotel, she jumps at the chance to escape her complicated life back on the mainland. She is, after all, the most hated woman in the country at present, and there's every chance the press will leave her alone when she's on New Zealand's smallest, most remote island, in the middle of winter.

The only catch? The other half of the hotel is owned by Ambrose, a cranky, uptight school teacher, and she hasn't made the best first impression. All Liv needs to do is lay low and help fix up the property to sell. Then her life can get back to normal.

Two sisters, one pen

"Sisters approaching middle age gracelessly, we are best friends, avid readers, writers and cocktail enthusiasts."

Kirsty lives in Auckland with her husband, three children and a menagerie of animals. Nikki has three adult children and divides her time between Tauranga and Wellington. Together they write light, humorous stories, the kind that get read in one sitting.

Their life goal is to be invited to a dinner party hosted by Harry Styles.
